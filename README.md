# pharr-fire-rescue-game
Intaractive quiz game for FD deployment of the Pharr Way

Play as Sparky, Ash, Blaze and Axel as they work together to put out fires that are spreading across City Hall.
The more questions you answer correct, the higher your combo multiplier score grows! Work as a team to put out all the fires.
If you can't put out all the fires, the highest scoring player wins!
